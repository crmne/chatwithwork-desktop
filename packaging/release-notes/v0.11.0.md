Notifications on your computer now say what happened: the chat's title or the bot's name, over the start of the answer or what's waiting for you. Hand-offs from bots and the Decisions digest reach your computer too, and the digest opens Decisions.

## New

- **Notifications that say something.** With **Show the chat and its answer** on in Settings, Notifications, a notification shows the chat's title or the bot's name and the start of the answer or what's waiting, so you can tell whether it needs you without opening anything. Turned off, it names the organization and what happened. Desktop keeps the words in memory only until the notification ends, and never logs or stores them. By @crmne ([#129](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/129)).
- **Hand-offs and the Decisions digest reach your computer.** Desktop used to drop these two kinds, so they only reached the browser. By @crmne ([#129](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/129)).
- **The Decisions digest opens Decisions.** When several decisions pile up, clicking the notification takes you to all of them, in the app or the browser, instead of the one chat that set it off. By @crmne ([#129](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/129)).

## Fixed

- **Dependency updates.** `zip` 9.2.2, a security update, and `log`. By @crmne ([#126](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/126), [#119](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/119)).

## Known limitations

- **The system keeps its own notification history.** Desktop doesn't control what your operating system's notification center holds on to.
- **Word writing still uses `zip` 8.** `docx-rs` hasn't released a version on `zip` 9. It only builds documents in memory ([details](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/blob/main/docs/zip-review.md)).
- **Printing hasn't been tried on a physical printer.**
- **Linux animations can still remain at 60 fps.**
- **The browser extension is still installed manually.**
- **Linux updates use the package manager or one-line installer.** On Mac, use the signed and notarized DMG; the PKG remains unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn while the installer builds reputation.
