The terminal app switches between your organizations with `/org`, hides its sidebar while slash commands still reach everything it held, keeps a bot's page up to date by itself, and wears the rainbow around its composer as the web does. In the browser, a click or a typed word that can't finish now says plainly whether anything reached the page, page readings show where links on your allowed sites go, and a link partly hidden under a bar can be clicked.

## New

- **Switch organizations from the terminal with `/org`.** It opens a picker of the organizations this computer is signed in to, with the open one marked, or takes a name or a number to switch at once. The switch starts the other organization's session on its own list, counts and Decisions, with nothing from the previous one carried over, and `cww tui` opens there next time. By @crmne ([#264](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/264)).
- **Hide the terminal's sidebar.** `[` or `/sidebar hide` gives the chat the full width, and it stays hidden next launch. Everything the sidebar holds has a command: `/new`, `/new-bot`, `/decisions`, `/search`, `/open`, `/resume`, `/bots`, `/projects`, `/project`, `/pin`, `/settings` and `/logout`. By @crmne ([#264](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/264)).
- **The terminal's composer wears the rainbow,** as the web's does: still at rest, flowing while an answer is written, dimmed while it can't send, and amber while a change or a question waits for you. It's drawn in your terminal's own colours, so 16-colour terminals and themes restyle it, and `NO_COLOR` keeps it plain. By @crmne ([#264](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/264)).
- **A bot's page in the terminal updates by itself** when a run starts, ends or asks something, keeping what you had selected. `r` or `/refresh` reads the screen again by hand. By @crmne ([#264](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/264)).

![The terminal app's /org picker over a chat: the open organization marked current, two others below it; from synthetic data](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/releases/download/v0.19.0/terminal-org.png)

- **Page readings show where links go, as far as Chat with Work could go anyway.** On a site you allowed, a link now reads `link "Style" [to=/admin/theme/style]`, so when a click fails it can open the address instead. Only the path is shown, never the query, the fragment or a login; parts that look like tokens or ids (long random runs, UUIDs, base64, hashes) show as `…`; and a link to a site you didn't allow, or one that isn't a web address, shows nothing at all, not even its host. By @crmne; thanks @crmne for asking for it ([#267](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/267), [#261](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/261)).

## Fixed

- **A browser action that can't finish says what happened.** A click, a typed word or a choice that didn't finish always answered "Its result is uncertain", even when nothing had reached the page. Now it says when nothing was sent and why (the element is disabled, hidden or covered, the page changed first, or you pressed Stop), and when something was sent, what the page looks like now: the same page, reloaded, gone elsewhere, the element gone, a dialog dismissed. So Chat with Work knows whether another try could do it twice. By @crmne; thanks @crmne for reporting it ([#266](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/266), [#262](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/262)).

![What Chat with Work hears back from two clicks that couldn't finish, before and now: "Nothing was clicked: the element didn't become ready in time (that element is disabled)", and "The click was sent, but the browser action didn't finish: the page stayed on the same address and document"; from a test with synthetic data](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/releases/download/v0.19.0/browser-answer.png)

- **A link partly hidden under a fixed bar can be clicked.** Clicks aimed only at an element's middle, so a bar drawing its handle across the middle of its links made every click on them fail, though most of each link showed. Chat with Work now clicks a part of the element that shows; one covered everywhere is still refused, by name, with nothing clicked. By @crmne; thanks @crmne for reporting it ([#263](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/263), [#260](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/260)).
- **Opening a site in a new tab no longer leaves it blank now and then.** On a busy computer the new tab could miss the address, stay empty, and say after 10 seconds that it had gone to a site you didn't allow. It now waits for the empty tab to be ready first. By @crmne ([#268](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/268)).

## Known limitations

- **The browser changes need extension 0.1.6.** The Chrome Web Store offers 0.1.5 until its review of 0.1.6 passes; the [manual ZIP](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/releases/tag/browser-v0.1.6) has it now. With 0.1.5, clicks, page readings and new tabs work as they did in 0.18.1.
- **Each part of a link's path is judged on its own** between its `/`s, so a token split across several short parts would still show.
- **`/org` lists only the organizations signed in on this computer.** Another one needs `cww login --organization` first.
- **After Chat with Work restarts, a computer can now and then say it's connected while chats see it offline,** until its background agent restarts ([#246](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/246)).
- **Another extension's popup over a field can leave a tab unreadable** ([#237](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/237)).
- **`cww roots` changes don't go through the daemon's ordered edit yet,** so a stalled change from the app can still undo one made in a terminal ([#239](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/239)).
- **On Windows, a pause pressed while a thinking step gets ready can miss it** ([#240](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/240)).
- **A folder that was missing when Desktop started stays "Folder not found" after it appears** until the background agent restarts ([#241](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/issues/241)).
