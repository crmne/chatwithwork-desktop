Chat with Work Desktop now shows its notifications on computers whose clock runs a little behind. Until now, a clock even slightly slow made every notification fail, and Chat with Work fell back to the browser.

## Fixed

- **Desktop notifications show on computers whose clock is slightly behind.** Chat with Work gives each notification 15 seconds to show before it uses the browser instead, and says when that time runs out. A computer whose clock was even a fraction of a second behind the server's read that as more than 15 seconds and turned every notification down, on any system. It now waits at most 15 seconds, whatever its clock says. By @crmne ([#32](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/pull/32)).

## Known limitations

- **A clock more than 15 seconds ahead of the server's still misses notifications**, which then go to the browser. Let your system set the time automatically.
- **Windows builds and the macOS `.pkg` remain unsigned.** The macOS `.dmg` is signed and notarized. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
- **The app does not update itself.** Upgrade through your package manager or download this release. After upgrading a Linux package, run `systemctl --user restart cww.service`.

The other limitations in [0.6.0's notes](https://github.com/crmne/chatwithwork-desktop/releases/tag/v0.6.0) still apply.
