TWAINDSM.dll

The scanner helper (cww-scan-helper.exe) loads TWAINDSM.dll, the TWAIN Data
Source Manager, to talk to scanners' TWAIN drivers. It is the TWAIN Working
Group's own 32-bit build, release 2.5.1, unmodified and signed by them:

  https://github.com/twain/twain-dsm/releases/tag/v2.5.1 (dsm_020501.zip,
  windows/32/TWAINDSM.dll)

Its source code is at https://github.com/twain/twain-dsm.

The TWAIN Data Source Manager is free software, licensed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License, version 2.1 (TWAINDSM-LICENSE.txt beside this file).
Chat with Work loads it as a separate library: you may replace TWAINDSM.dll
in this folder with another build of it, including one you made from its
source, and the scanner helper will use it.
